name: PIPEDA Compliance Gate
on: [push, pull_request]

jobs:
  privacy-lint:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - uses: actions/checkout@v4
      - name: Install pipedact
        run: curl -sSL https://get.pipedact.dev | sh
      - name: Run Privacy Linter
        run: pipedact check ./src --strict --format sarif --out audit.sarif
      - name: Upload SARIF report
        uses: github/codeql-action/upload-sarif@v3
        if: always()
        with:
          sarif_file: audit.sarif

PIPEDA Schedule 1 — 10 Fair Information Principles Technical Map

How pipedact maps federal Canadian statutory requirements to automated static analysis rules, AST queries, and automated testing hooks.

Principle & Statute Statutory Meaning Automated Technical Gate in pipedact Severity
1. Accountability Designating a Privacy Officer and auditing 3rd-party transfers. Audits external API URLs & SDK calls to unvetted sub-processors. MEDIUM
2. Identifying Purposes Specify why information is collected before or at collection. Rejects database migration or schema without @privacy(purpose) tags. HIGH
3. Consent Knowledge and meaningful consent before processing personal data. Enforces conditional consent guard wrapper on tracking & marketing triggers. HIGH
4. Limiting Collection Collect only what is necessary for declared purposes (Minimization). Detects over-collection fields (e.g., requesting SIN during registration). HIGH
5. Retention & Disposal Keep personal info only as long as necessary, then destroy or anonymize. Ensures DB models declare TTL indexes or cron deletion cascades. MEDIUM
6. Accuracy Keep personal data as accurate, complete and up-to-date as necessary. Validates regex & format validators (e.g. Luhn algorithm for SIN). LOW
7. Safeguards Protect information against loss, unauthorized access, copying, disclosure. Blocks raw PII flowing to stdout, server logs, or unencrypted cookies. CRITICAL
8. Openness Make clear information about privacy policies readily available. Checks presence of public privacy endpoints and versioned policy routes. LOW
9. Individual Access Upon request, inform individuals of existence, use, and disclosure + export. Audits user data export routines to ensure all stored tables are included. MEDIUM
10. Challenging Compliance Address challenges and complaints concerning compliance. Verifies privacy dispute contact points or ticketing integration routes. INFO
